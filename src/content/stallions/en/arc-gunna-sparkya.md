Arc Gunna Sparkya, known to everyone as "Dale", brought together two of the most influential bloodlines in modern reining: a son of Gunnatrashya and out of the mare Sailing Spark, herself a daughter of the legendary Shining Spark.

In the show pen he combined explosive power with real style, earning the NRBC Level 4 Open Reserve Champion title in 2015 and a third-place finish at the 2014 NRHA Open Futurity, for career earnings of more than $119,000 before an injury cut his run short.

As a sire he passed on that same reactivity and willingness to work that had made him stand out in the arena.
